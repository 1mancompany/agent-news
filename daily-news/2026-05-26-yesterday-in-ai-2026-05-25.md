## 1⃣ Anthropic Closing In on $900B Crown — Largest-Ever Private AI Fundraise

- **What:** Anthropic is finalizing a $30B+ funding round at a pre-money valuation above $900 billion, expected to close the week of May 26, 2026.
- **Why it matters:** At a $930B post-money figure, Anthropic would surpass OpenAI's $852B valuation from March 2026 to become the world's most valuable private AI company.
- **Key number:** $930B — projected post-money valuation surpassing OpenAI.

The round is co-led by Sequoia Capital, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks, each committing roughly $2 billion. Founders Fund and General Catalyst are among existing investors also expected to participate. This follows Anthropic's $30 billion Series G in February 2026 at a $380 billion valuation — meaning the company has nearly tripled its valuation in roughly 14 weeks.

The financing reflects explosive revenue growth: Q1 2026 hit $4.8 billion, and Q2 is tracking to $10.9 billion, more than the company's entire 2025 annual revenue. Anthropic projects an annualized run rate of $50 billion by end of June 2026, driven primarily by Claude Code and enterprise API adoption. The company also expects to report its first operating profit in Q2, though critics note the calculation excludes a $1.25 billion per month SpaceX compute contract.

## 2⃣ 18-Minute VS Code Extension Heist Breaches GitHub, OpenAI, and Mistral AI

- **What:** Threat actor TeamPCP pushed a malicious build of the Nx Console VS Code extension (v18.95.0) to the Visual Studio Marketplace on May 18; it was live for exactly 18 minutes and drained ~3,800 GitHub internal repositories.
- **Why it matters:** The attack shows how a single compromised developer extension — even briefly available — can propagate a credential-stealing payload across the entire AI industry supply chain.
- **Key number:** 18 minutes — the window TeamPCP's extension was publicly available before detection.

The campaign started May 11 when TeamPCP compromised TanStack's npm ecosystem across 170 packages, then used access from a Narwhal Technologies developer to push a poisoned Nx Console build. The credential stealer harvested 1Password vaults, Claude Code configurations, npm tokens, GitHub tokens, and AWS credentials from any developer who auto-updated in that window.

GitHub confirmed ~3,800 internal repos were exfiltrated. OpenAI disclosed two employee devices were compromised with a subset of internal source code accessed, and is revoking its macOS app signing certificate on June 12 as a direct response. Mistral AI is a third confirmed victim. TeamPCP listed stolen data on cybercrime forums with asks above $50,000.

## 3⃣ Claude Gets 28 Enterprise Guards: Anthropic Launches Compliance API

- **What:** Anthropic released the Claude Compliance API on May 25, connecting Claude Enterprise to 28 security and compliance platforms including CrowdStrike, Datadog, Microsoft Purview, Okta, Palo Alto Networks, Snyk, Wiz, and Zscaler.
- **Why it matters:** Enterprise IT teams can now route Claude conversation logs and activity events into their existing SIEM, DLP, and identity management stacks without custom integration work.
- **Key number:** 28 — security vendors integrated at day-one launch.

The Compliance API exposes two data streams: conversation content from Claude Enterprise (chats, uploaded files, and projects) and platform activity events. Any organization already running one of the 28 launch partners can connect their Claude instance and have usage data flowing into existing dashboards and alerting workflows immediately.

Partners span DLP, SASE, data security, SIEM, security operations, identity management, eDiscovery, AI security posture management, and observability. Notable names include IBM Guardium, Proofpoint, ReliaQuest, Rubrik, SailPoint, Smarsh, Sumo Logic, Tenable, Trellix, Varonis, Fortinet, Mimecast, Cyera, Cribl, and Cloudflare CASB. The launch positions Claude as a first-class citizen in enterprise security stacks.
