## 1⃣ Google's Gemini 2.5 Pro Deep Think Launches, Ties GPT-5.4 on Day One

- **What:** Google released Gemini 2.5 Pro with a Deep Think reasoning mode on June 22, 2026
- **Why it matters:** It's the first model to match GPT-5.4 in human-preference rankings while offering twice the context window of any competitor
- **Key number:** 2,000,000 — the token context window, double the nearest rival

Google's Gemini 2.5 Pro with Deep Think landed on June 22 and immediately rewrote benchmark comparisons. The model scores 78% on SWE-bench — behind GPT-5's 80% and DeepSeek V4's 81% but ahead of Claude Sonnet 4.6's 73% — while tieing GPT-5.4 for first on LMArena, the human-preference leaderboard that measures blind side-by-side comparisons. On competitive coding (LiveCodeBench V6) and expert-level science (Humanity's Last Exam), Gemini 2.5 Deep Think claims state-of-the-art results.

The headline differentiator is context: 2 million tokens, double what any other frontier model currently supports. Deep Think mode explores multiple solution paths before responding, yielding 5–15% performance gains over standard output on hard math and multi-step coding problems. It's available immediately through Google AI Studio and the Gemini API.

## 2⃣ Anthropic Will Scan Your Face to Use Claude Starting July 8

- **What:** Anthropic announced mandatory biometric ID verification for some Claude consumer users, effective July 8, 2026
- **Why it matters:** It's the first time a major AI lab has required government-issued ID plus facial biometrics as a condition of service for free and paid consumer users
- **Key number:** July 8 — the date the policy takes effect, requiring government photo ID, live selfie, and facial geometry scan

TechCrunch reported on June 22 that Anthropic's identity verification policy — first applied in limited form since April 14, 2026 — formally expands on July 8. Affected users must provide a physical government-issued document (passport, driver's license, or national ID; photocopies and digital IDs rejected) plus a live selfie that generates a facial geometry scan. Data is held by third-party vendor Persona Identities, not on Anthropic's own systems.

The policy applies only to Claude Free, Pro, and Max subscribers — Team, Enterprise, and API customers are exempt. Anthropic says verification prevents abuse, enforces usage policies, and meets legal obligations, and pledges the data will not train models. User pushback has been significant, with critics flagging the scope of biometric data collected and unclear triggers for who gets prompted.

## 3⃣ OpenMontage: 500+ Agent Skills, Zero Human Editors Required

- **What:** calesthio/OpenMontage debuted on GitHub as the self-described first open-source agentic video production system
- **Why it matters:** It turns a single prompt into a researched, scripted, and fully produced video — end to end, without human intervention
- **Key number:** 500+ — distinct agent skills bundled in the framework across 12 production pipelines and 52 tools

OpenMontage hit GitHub trending on June 20 and held #2 on June 22. The project bundles 12 specialized production pipelines (research, scripting, scene planning, asset generation, editing, and more) with 52 integrated tools and over 500 agent skills. Before writing a line of script, the agent autonomously searches YouTube, Reddit, Hacker News, and academic sources to gather data and trending angles — then generates and assembles the final video end-to-end.

Where earlier agentic video tools handled one or two steps, OpenMontage covers the entire production stack. It's designed to work alongside any AI coding assistant and targets creators, marketers, and developers who need high-volume video output without a production team behind it.
