## 1️⃣ Anthropic Opens Seoul Office While Fable 5 Remains Grounded

- **What:** On June 19 Anthropic launched its third Asia-Pacific office in Seoul, announcing Claude deployments at Naver, LG CNS, and Samsung SDS — the same day TechCrunch argued the US export ban on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 is historically doomed to fail.
- **Why it matters:** A US AI company simultaneously expanding in a market while its best models are blocked from that market's users illustrates the impossible bind AI export controls create for global SaaS businesses.
- **Key number:** ~150 — the organizations in Project Glasswing with Mythos 5 access before the ban; SK Telecom was among them.

The ban, in effect since June 12, bars all foreign nationals from Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models. The trigger: SK Telecom — Korea's largest carrier and a $100M Anthropic investor — was flagged by the White House as a Chinese security risk. Amazon separately warned of a bypass vector in Fable 5, escalating a targeted access revocation into a blanket global ban.

TechCrunch's June 19 analysis argued software export controls have never held — citing Zimmermann/PGP (1991) and NSO Group's Pegasus as historical failures. Anthropic's international managing director told the Seoul crowd he was "very confident" both models would return "in the coming days."

## 2️⃣ MCP Gets Zero-Touch Enterprise SSO — Okta Ships First

- **What:** The Model Context Protocol's Enterprise-Managed Authorization (EMA) extension reached stable status on June 18, replacing per-user OAuth prompts with IdP-delegated access that activates automatically on first corporate login.
- **Why it matters:** Per-user OAuth consent was the single biggest barrier to enterprise MCP rollouts; stable EMA makes MCP server access as routine as a SaaS entitlement in an IAM system.
- **Key number:** 7 MCP server providers ship EMA at launch (Asana, Atlassian, Canva, Figma, Granola, Linear, Supabase); Slack is next.

Okta leads the identity provider charge, using its Cross-App Access protocol and Identity Assertion JWT grants. When an employee logs in to Claude, VS Code, or any EMA-capable client, every MCP server their IdP group allows connects automatically — no separate flows, no individual consent screens.

Anthropic and Microsoft VS Code are the first clients. The MCP spec, stewarded by the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation since December 2025, formally incorporated the authorization extension in November 2025; June 18 moved it from draft to stable.

## 3️⃣ EU Picks EUROPA to Build a 400B+ Open-Source AI in 24 Languages

- **What:** The European Commission selected the EUROPA consortium, led by Italian firm Domyn, to build Europe's first open-source frontier AI model — more than 400 billion parameters, covering all 24 official EU languages, with one year of EuroHPC compute access.
- **Why it matters:** A 400B+ open model covering low-resource EU languages like Maltese and Irish would give European institutions a sovereign alternative to US and Chinese frontier models for the first time.
- **Key number:** 400B+ parameters — the minimum scale required by the Grand Challenge specification, comparable to GPT-4-class architectures.

Launched in February 2026, the Frontier AI Grand Challenge was the Commission's largest-ever AI procurement. EUROPA beat competing European consortia; Domyn leads the group and must release weights under an open license, with EuroHPC supercomputers running the training.

The announcement marks a strategic turn: Europe is no longer only regulating foreign AI (EU AI Act) but funding domestic frontier development with public compute. Covering all 24 official languages means handling low-resource cases that every current frontier model underserves.
