## 1️⃣ Anthropic Expects Its First-Ever Profit — $559M Operating Income on $10.9B Revenue

- **What:** Anthropic shared internal projections with investors showing Q2 2026 revenue of $10.9B (up 130% from Q1's $4.8B) and a first-ever operating profit of $559M.
- **Why it matters:** Anthropic has burned billions since 2021; a profitable quarter validates the enterprise-Claude bet and sets up the company's next funding round on dramatically stronger terms.
- **Key number:** 130% — quarter-over-quarter revenue growth rate, Q1 to Q2 2026.

The figures were first reported by the Wall Street Journal on May 20 and confirmed across multiple outlets by May 21. Anthropic cautioned that it may not sustain profitability for the full year as planned infrastructure spending accelerates, but the Q2 projection signals that enterprise adoption — particularly Claude Code and the cybersecurity pipeline from Project Glasswing partners — is converting to cash at an unprecedented pace.

Q1 came in at $4.8B, up from roughly $1.6B annualized in mid-2025, reflecting large enterprise deals with AWS, Apple, and Microsoft alongside rapid Claude Code uptake. The swing to $559M operating income represents a complete reversal of a loss-making streak that began at the company's founding in 2021.

## 2️⃣ Trump Killed an AI Executive Order Hours Before the Signing Ceremony

- **What:** The White House pulled a planned executive order on May 21 that would have created a voluntary framework giving the federal government up to 90 days of pre-release access to frontier AI models, including NSA involvement in classified security testing.
- **Why it matters:** It was the Trump administration's first attempt to build any oversight mechanism for powerful AI — and its last-minute collapse leaves the U.S. with no framework for reviewing models before they ship to the public.
- **Key number:** 90 — days labs would have voluntarily shared models with the government before public release.

Trump told reporters he "didn't like certain aspects" and that the U.S. is ahead of China on AI and he didn't want to do anything threatening that lead. The draft order had two sections: a cybersecurity clearinghouse between Treasury, other agencies, and AI companies; and a "covered frontier models" section defining which systems qualified for early government review. OpenAI and Anthropic had both been in active negotiations with the White House prior to the withdrawal. The administration has no announced timeline for a replacement framework. It revoked Biden's AI executive order in January 2025.

## 3️⃣ Nvidia Posts $81.6B Quarter — Best in Tech History — and Guides to $91B

- **What:** Nvidia reported Q1 FY2027 revenue of $81.6B (+85% YoY), guided for Q2 at $91B (±2%), and authorized an additional $80B share repurchase while raising its quarterly dividend 25x.
- **Why it matters:** The Data Center segment alone — $75.2B — is now larger than the entire annual revenue of most Fortune 100 companies, putting the infrastructure cost of the AI wave in concrete terms.
- **Key number:** $75.2B — data center revenue alone, up 92% year-over-year.

Net income reached $58.32B ($2.39/share), up from $18.78B in the year-ago quarter. Q2 guidance of $91B topped Wall Street's $86.84B estimate by 5%. The Blackwell 300 ramp and NVLink/InfiniBand demand from hyperscalers drove most of the beat; hyperscale held roughly 50% of Data Center revenue, with the other 50% split across AI clouds, enterprise, industrial, and sovereign customers. One constraint: Nvidia shipped zero Hopper (H100/H200) GPUs to China — down from $4.6B in Q1 FY2026 — following U.S. export restrictions. The company holds H200 licenses for specific China customers but has not yet recognized any revenue under the program.
