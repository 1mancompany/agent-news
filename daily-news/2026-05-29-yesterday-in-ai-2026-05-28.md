## 1️⃣ Anthropic Raises $65B — Surpasses OpenAI at $965B Valuation

- **What:** Anthropic closed a $65B Series H round — expected to be its last private raise before an IPO — at a $965B post-money valuation.
- **Why it matters:** The round makes Anthropic the most valuable private AI company in the world, eclipsing rival OpenAI for the first time.
- **Key number:** $47B — Anthropic's annualised revenue run rate as of May 2026.

The round was co-led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital, with further participation from Capital Group, Coatue, GIC, Blackstone, Fidelity, Temasek, and others. It folds in $15B of previously committed hyperscaler investments, including $5B from Amazon.

Anthropic says it will use the funds to advance safety and interpretability research, expand compute, and scale its product suite. The company projects a 130% revenue surge this year and expects its first operating profit, setting the stage for a public market debut. Both Anthropic and OpenAI are reported to be eyeing an IPO as soon as 2026 to fund the compute needed for the next generation of frontier models.

## 2️⃣ Claude Opus 4.8: 4× Fewer Unflagged Bugs, Dynamic Workflows With Hundreds of Subagents

- **What:** Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 4.8 on May 28 — 41 days after Opus 4.7 — scoring 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified and introducing dynamic workflows for codebase-scale agentic tasks.
- **Why it matters:** Dynamic workflows let a single Claude Code session plan a large task, spawn hundreds of parallel subagents, and self-verify outputs before returning results — enabling full-repo migrations end to end.
- **Key number:** 4× — Opus 4.8 is four times less likely to leave code defects it generated unremarked, versus Opus 4.7.

Alignment scores now sit near Claude Mythos Preview levels: rates of deception and misuse cooperation are substantially lower than Opus 4.7, making this the most honest non-Mythos model Anthropic has shipped. A new effort slider (four levels: low, default, extra, max) lets users trade compute for speed; Opus 4.8 at minimum effort already matches Opus 4.7 at maximum.

Fast mode now runs 2.5× faster at one-third the previous cost — $10/M input and $50/M output tokens. Prompt caching cuts costs by up to 90%; batch processing by 50%. Anthropic says Mythos-class access is coming to all customers "in the coming weeks."

## 3️⃣ Mistral Renames Le Chat to Vibe, Lands Airbus + BMW, Plans 13,800-GPU Paris Cluster

- **What:** At its first AI Now Summit on May 28, Mistral AI rebranded Le Chat as Vibe, launched a manufacturing-focused AI platform, and announced an $830M debt-funded data center south of Paris.
- **Why it matters:** Mistral is repositioning from model lab to full-stack AI company — with Airbus, BMW, and EDF as headline customers and a European GPU cluster that rivals hyperscaler build-outs.
- **Key number:** 13,800 — Nvidia GB300 GPUs in the planned Bruyères-le-Châtel facility, delivering 44 MW of compute capacity.

Vibe turns Le Chat into a unified agent platform: it manages inbox, calendar, deep research, drafting, and long-running coding tasks across web, editor, and terminal. The industrial platform — built on Emmi AI simulation technology acquired earlier in May — targets aerospace, automotive, and semiconductor firms, applying LLMs alongside physics simulation to design validation and production optimisation. Airbus, BMW Group, and EDF are flagship customers at launch.

Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch told CNBC the company is exploring custom chip design. With 1,000 employees and a €1B revenue target for 2026, Mistral is the most credible European challenger to US frontier labs.
